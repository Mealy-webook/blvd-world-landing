Explore rides

Get ready for an exciting adventure with rides. including Sky-loop, Trubo 360, The wheel, Rollover, and more.

Wave Swinger

Thrill: Max
Height: 130-195cm

A gentle-looking swing that picks up speed until the whole zone tilts sideways. Everyone’s first ride of the night.

Sky Loop

Thrill: Max
Height: 140-195cm

A vertical loop that flips you twice before you can decide whether to scream. The queue is half the fun — the drop is all of it.

Fuji River Rapids

Thrill: Medium
Height: 110-195cm

A splashing flume run through the canyon that soaks the first two rows. Sit at the back if you value dry shoes.

Turbo 360

Thrill: High
Height: 135-195cm

Three hundred and sixty degrees of pure spin, suspended over the lake. Keep your eyes open on the way over the top.

The Wheel

Thrill: Gentle
Height: 90-195cm

The calmest seat in the park and the best view of all twenty zones lit up after dark. Bring someone you like.

Rollover

Thrill: High
Height: 140-195cm

Swings out, tips over and holds you there just long enough to regret your dinner order. A BLVD World favourite.

King Claw

Thrill: Max
Height: 145-195cm

Meet the beast that’s changing the thrill game. King Claw has arrived and it’s every bit as monstrous as you imagined (in the best possible way).
